# Social posts for the launch videos

Game link: https://www.roblox.com/games/135511260983800

The owner posts these. Music is "A Game" from Mixkit (see MUSIC_LICENSE.md). Never register it with
Content ID. On TikTok, upload the file with its own audio; don't swap in a TikTok library sound.

## Vertical short (`dig-to-the-core-vertical.mp4`): TikTok, YouTube Shorts, Instagram Reels

Post the same video on all three. Short hooks work best; the first second has to stop the scroll.

**Caption option A**
> how deep can you dig? 🕳️⛏️ Dig to the Core! is free on Roblox
> #roblox #robloxgames #robloxsimulator #dig #pets #fyp

**Caption option B**
> POV: you hit a PERFECT tap 993m underground 😳 #roblox #robloxfyp #simulator #gaming

**Caption option C**
> what's buried under the beach?? 🏖️ (link in bio) #roblox #newrobloxgame #robloxpets

- **TikTok / Reels:** put the game link in the bio, because links in captions aren't clickable. Pin a
  comment: "Search Dig to the Core! on Roblox".
- **YouTube Shorts title:** `How Deep Can You Dig? ⛏️ #roblox #shorts`. Put the game link in the
  description.
- **When to post:** 3–6 pm US Eastern on a weekday, or weekend mornings. Post one a day for a week,
  reusing cuts of the same footage. Accounts that post consistently get pushed more.
