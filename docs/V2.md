# v2 Contract: Open Beach, Survival, Diggers, Polish

Binding for every v2 agent. This extends `docs/ARCHITECTURE.md`. Where the two disagree, this
file wins. If you need to break it, say so in your report rather than diverging silently.

## Owner decisions (from playtesting)
- **No plots.** The whole beach is one shared diggable area. All digging happens on the
  **water side** of the beach, in a long strip along the shoreline. Make the coastline longer.
  The hub, shops and boardwalk sit inland, on the +Z side.
- **Hole cleanup:** the tide refills holes. A dug area refills from the bottom up once no player
  has been near it for about 3–5 minutes. The High Tide event smooths the whole beach, but it
  never buries a player: anyone in a hole is lifted out first.
- **Depth gating stays:** deeper layers need better shovels or diggers (Hardness vs Power).
- **Beginner dig size:** the starter shovels dig a bit smaller than now. Aim halfway between the
  original 2-stud sphere (invisible) and the current 4×4×8 voxel cube. Digs must stay clearly
  visible. Bigger shovels scale up from there.
- **Heat:** a soft slowdown. A Heat meter rises in the sun. At `SunburntAt` the player becomes
  "Sunburnt" and digs at `1/SunburntCooldownMultiplier` speed until heat falls to `RecoverAt`.
  There is never damage or death. Things that cool you: being under shade, being underground,
  drinks and food. New players get a grace period.
- **Shade:** players buy shade items (umbrella → tarp → canopy → beach tent …). They place one
  on the beach surface and it cools **anyone** standing under it. It despawns when the owner
  leaves or places another one.
- **Consumables:** water is free at a hub fountain (`DrinkFountain`). Lemonade, coconut,
  popsicle, watermelon and so on cost coins. They cool you, and the better ones grant a short
  boost.
- **Diggers:** a separate companion category, independent of pets. They are bought and upgraded
  in a **Garage** at the hub. Equipped diggers follow the player and auto-dig near them.
  Sand goes into the player's backpack, which still respects capacity. Slots: 1 base, +1 from the
  AutoDig pass, and more from rebirth milestones (the Diggers agent decides). The AutoDig pass is
  redefined as "+1 digger slot and you auto-swing".
- **Max players: 16.**

## Interfaces between agents
**Beach → everyone**
- `World.GetDigZone(): { CFrame: CFrame, Size: Vector3 }`. The CFrame is at the centre of the
  flat dig surface (Y = `Config.SURFACE_Y`), axis-aligned. Size is the X/Z footprint plus the
  total depth in Y.
- `World.IsInDigZone(position: Vector3): boolean`.
- `DigService.DigAt(player, position, opts): Types.DigResult?`, where
  `opts = { Power: number, Radius: number, SandMultiplier: number, Source: "Shovel"|"Digger"|"Auto", Silent: boolean? }`.
  Diggers use this, so validation, backpack capacity, treasure rolls, terrain carving, the tide
  bookkeeping and quests all run through one path.
- **Player attribute `Depth`** (studs below the surface). It is 0 outside the dig zone or when
  above the surface.
- The beach surface spawn and return-to-surface point is `World.GetSurfacePoint(near: Vector3): CFrame`.
- Remove `PlotAssigned` and `GetPlot` from Remotes, along with every client use of them. The
  Beach agent may edit `Remotes.luau` to do this.

**Survival → Beach**
- The **player attribute `Sunburnt`** (boolean) is set by SurvivalService. DigService multiplies
  the dig cooldown by `Config.Heat.SunburntCooldownMultiplier` while it is true.

**Survival ← Beach**
- SurvivalService listens to `ServerSignals.Dug(player, depth, gained)` for dig heat, and
  reads the `Depth` attribute for underground cooling.

**Player attributes Survival sets:** `Heat` (0..Max), `Sunburnt` (bool), `InShade` (bool).

**Diggers**
- The player attribute `EquippedDiggers` holds comma-separated digger ids.
- The server fires `DiggerDug` to all clients for effects.
- DiggerService owns the dig loop and calls `DigService.DigAt`.

**HUD (Polish)** adds two menu buttons:
- **"Beach"** opens Panel `"BeachShop"`, the shade and food shop owned by Survival.
- **"Garage"** opens Panel `"Garage"`, owned by Diggers.

The two panel owners must register their panels under exactly those names
(`Panel.new({ Name = "BeachShop", ... })`).
The **SurvivalHUD** draws the heat meter and consumable quick-slots in its own ScreenGui frame
and must not overlap the main HUD. Polish documents the free screen regions in its report and
leaves the **right-centre** edge free for SurvivalHUD.

    Survival and Diggers agents each add their own small `describe` block. The mock is in
    `tests/mock/Roblox.luau`, and you may extend it.
- Learned in real Studio playtests, and all of it must be respected:
  - Smooth terrain's visible surface sits about 2 studs above voxel boundaries. Anything placed
    "on the ground" must sit at the visible surface, and triggers must not rely on `Touched`
    with thin buried pads.
  - Never trust a client click position to be inside solid terrain; resolve it on the server.
  - `Terrain.Decoration` is not scriptable.
  - Two copies of a script once ran, so keep the `shared` guards in the Main scripts.
- Do not git commit. The coordinator commits.

## v2.1: companions merge and ride-on excavator (binding)

Owner decisions:
- **The Garage and the separate Digger system are removed.** Construction vehicles become
  **pets** hatched from **Construction Crates**. Crates are `Config.Eggs` entries with
  `Kind = "Crate"` placed beside the eggs, and the egg arc builds a pedestal for each one.
- **Every pet keeps its sand multiplier.** Vehicle pets, and some creature pets (Mole, Crab, …),
  also get a `Dig` ability (`Types.PetDigDef`).
- **One pet inventory and one equip limit.**
- **Digging pets dig spots in a ring around the owner, roughly 6–12 studs away.** Each pet gets
  its own spot. They must **never dig under the owner's feet** and never teleport; they drive
  or walk to their spot.
- **Ride-on excavator.** It is a Mythic pet with `Rideable = true`, and it has about a 0.5% drop
  rate from the top Construction Crate. The player can ride it around the beach, and it digs
  big chunks in front of itself.
- **Old saves.** Existing `data.Diggers` entries convert to the matching vehicle pets once,
  through a DataService migration. After that, `data.Diggers` stays empty.
